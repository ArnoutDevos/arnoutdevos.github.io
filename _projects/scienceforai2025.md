Together with [Matthias Bethge](https://bethgelab.org) (Tübingen AI Center, ELLIS co-founder), I curated the *Science for AI* thematic workshop at the [AI in Science Summit 2025](https://ais25.eu) (AIS25) hosted by the University of Copenhagen. Over two half-days, we brought together researchers, founders, investors, and policymakers — including the Director of the EU AI Office — to discuss opportunities for European research excellence, public AI for science, and how to lead in AI innovation. The Sciencepreneurship Award was also presented during the workshop.

Original workshop page: [ais25.eu/thematic-workshope/science-for-ai-tech](https://ais25.eu/thematic-workshope/science-for-ai-tech)
