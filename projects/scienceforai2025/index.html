Science for AI Workshop @ AIS25

Two-day workshop at the AI in Science Summit 2025 in Copenhagen, curated with Matthias Bethge for ELLIS and the ELIAS Alliance. Researchers, founders, investors, and the EU AI Office on AI for science and innovation.

Together with Matthias Bethge (Tübingen AI Center, ELLIS co-founder), I curated the Science for AI thematic workshop at the AI in Science Summit 2025 (AIS25) hosted by the University of Copenhagen. Over two half-days, we brought together researchers, founders, investors, and policymakers — including the Director of the EU AI Office — to discuss opportunities for European research excellence, public AI for science, and how to lead in AI innovation. The Sciencepreneurship Award was also presented during the workshop.

Original workshop page: ais25.eu/thematic-workshope/science-for-ai-tech
