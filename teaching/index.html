Large-Scale AI Engineering

MS/PhD/PostDoc · 3 ECTS credits · Lecturer

I co-founded this hands-on course on large-scale AI with Imanol Schlag, focusing on the engineering principles and practices required to develop and optimize large-scale AI systems. It provides unique hands-on experience on the (Spring 2025) world’s most powerful public AI supercomputer (Alps).

Technology Investing

MS/MA/PhD/PostDoc · 2 ECTS credits · Lecturer

Venture Capital is important to fund big transformational ideas and is often misunderstood by tech or research entrepreneurs. In small teams, participants work on a case of a real startup and defend the case in a simulated investment committee (IC) consisting of experienced VCs.
