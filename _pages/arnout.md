👋 Hi, I'm Arnout, Lecturer in AI for Research at [ETH AI Center](https://ai.ethz.ch) ([ETH Zurich](https://ethz.ch/en.html)). Next to [teaching](/teaching/), I develop large-scale AI systems that do research autonomously, including an [⚛️ AI Scientist](/projects/aiscientist/), recasting invention itself as a problem we can engineer. Get in touch with a hard problem, domain expertise, or a check — or to simply explore working together.

Previously, I led Science at <a href="https://ellis.eu" class="ellis-wordmark"><span>E</span><span>L</span><span>L</span><span>I</span><span>S</span></a>; obtained my PhD in ML from <a href="https://www.epfl.ch/" style="color:red;">EPFL</a> as a [Marie-Curie](https://marie-sklodowska-curie-actions.ec.europa.eu/) fellow, including internships at <a href="https://research.atspotify.com/" style="color:green;">Spotify</a>, <a href="https://www.amazon.science/" style="color:darkorange;">Amazon</a>, and <a href="https://bmir.stanford.edu/" style="color:#E74C3C;">Stanford University</a>; co-founded an AI EdTech startup; and built up the student Pre-Seed VC fund [S2S Ventures](https://s2s.vc), whose first 4 investments I led — so far yielding 1 exit ([Digit Soil](https://www.digit-soil.com/)), a [$40M Series A](https://x.com/adaptyvbio/status/2092324587342303697) ([Adaptyv Bio](https://www.adaptyvbio.com/)), and a [€4M Seed round](https://techfundingnews.com/maia-prodlane-4m-funding-industrial-knowledge-ai/) ([MAIA](https://en.getmaia.ai/)). Earlier: MSc CS from <a href="https://www.usc.edu" style="color:#E74C3C;">USC</a> 🇺🇸 as a [BAEF](https://baef.be/baef-fellows/#2017) fellow, and degrees in Management, EE, and EECS from <a href="https://www.kuleuven.be/english/kuleuven/" style="color:#3498DB;">KU Leuven</a> 🇧🇪.

Alongside this, I enjoy learning and building with peers, which led me to co-found (1) the [ETH AI Club](https://www.ethaiclub.org/), ETH Zurich's students & staff volunteer association on AI with 250+ members, and (2) the global [Sciencepreneurship Community](https://sciencepreneurship.ch) of entrepreneurial scientists, whose alumni include the founders of [Prior Labs](https://priorlabs.ai) ([€1B+ exit to SAP](https://tech.eu/2026/07/17/sap-acquires-prior-labs-just-18-months-after-launch-in-eur1b-deal/)) & [UNOMR](https://unomr.com/).

CV on request. Although I'm a native Dutch 🇳🇱 speaker with proficient French 🇫🇷 and some German 🇩🇪, I strongly prefer communicating in English 🇺🇸. Open to consulting & advising.
